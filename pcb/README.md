# KiCad hardware files

Open `Synchronous_Buck.kicad_pro` in KiCad 8 or newer. The complete schematic is on one A3 sheet. Open `Synchronous_Buck.kicad_pcb` in PCB Editor and press Alt+3 for the 3D viewer.

Keep `Buck.kicad_sym`, `Buck_Footprints.pretty`, `3dmodels`, `fp-lib-table` and `sym-lib-table` beside the project. The board uses two copper layers, a 166 × 53 mm outline, 1.6 mm FR4 and 35 µm / 1 oz copper as the fabrication specification.

`Manufacturing/` contains the copper, mask, silkscreen, outline and plated/non-plated drill outputs. `Synchronous_Buck_Gerbers_Drills.zip` groups the ten fabrication files. `Board_Assembly.csv` lists component references, parts and fit status; `Test_Points.csv` lists measurement points.
